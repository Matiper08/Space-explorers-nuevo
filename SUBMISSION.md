## Summary

Maia and Tuerca follow the echoes of space hardware across three continuous worlds: the Moon, Mars, and deep space. At 14 mission stations, players try a short activity inspired by how an explorer operated, then read a mission card about its purpose, operation, discoveries, and current status. The journey treats these machines as part of a human story while helping young players see that “forgotten” does not always mean “left behind.”

The game is designed for children ages 6–12. Its tone is gentle, its Grays are transformed into colorful sparks rather than harmed, and Tuerca rescues Maia instead of using a game-over screen.

## Learning and design

- The Moon features the Eagle Lunar Module, an Apollo rover, ALSEP, and Surveyor 3.
- Mars features Pathfinder, Spirit and Opportunity, Viking 1, InSight, and Ingenuity.
- Deep space features Dawn, NEAR Shoemaker, Pioneer 10, Kepler, and Voyager 1 and 2.
- Mission cards link to the NASA sources listed in `CREDITS.md`; unsupported claims are marked `// VERIFY` in the data.
- English is the default language; Spanish is available. The game supports keyboard, pointer, and on-screen touch controls.

## Technology and disclosure
