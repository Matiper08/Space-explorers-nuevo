# Demo Script

Suggested walkthrough: about 3 minutes. Use the English interface for the jury; `L` switches to Spanish.

## 0:00–0:25 — Introduce the idea

On the title screen, introduce Maia, Tuerca, and the Ecoscope: a radio that follows the stories of space explorers. Explain the central idea: forgotten does not always mean abandoned.

## 0:25–0:45 — Choose a world

Open the system map from the menu. Point out the Moon, Mars, and deep-space destinations, and note that this is a story map, not a scale model. Select the Moon and begin the journey.

## 0:45–1:20 — Show movement and controls

Advance Elena's opening dialogue. Demonstrate movement, a jump, and a double jump. Point out the echo-star, battery, and mission-progress HUD, plus the labeled on-screen controls. Press `P` to pause and `P` again to resume. Press `Esc` to return to the main menu.

## 1:20–2:10 — Meet a mission station

Walk to the Apollo 11 Eagle station, climb the nearby platforms, and interact as prompted. Read the mission card and point out its NASA source link. Explain how each station turns a piece of mission history into a short playable activity.

## 2:10–2:35 — Show the Journal and language option

Return to the menu, open the Ecoscope Journal, and show how recovered mission cards are saved in the browser. Use `L` to briefly demonstrate Spanish, then switch back to English.

## 2:35–3:00 — Close with the wider journey

Use the system map to show that the same idea continues on Mars and in deep space, ending with Voyager 1 and 2. Mention that the mission facts link to NASA pages and that claims needing more source support are marked `// VERIFY`.
