# Credits

## Project

**Space Explorers: The Quiet Signal** was created by a student team for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026. Team member names are intentionally not listed in this public file.

The characters Maia, Tuerca, Elena, the Grays, dialogue, game code, and procedural visual designs were created for this project, with AI assistance disclosed in `AI_DISCLOSURE.md`. LittleBigPlanet was considered only as a broad reference for warmth and playful exploration; no characters, names, art, music, audio, or other assets from that game are used.
